Data: RAWS observations, forecasts and NFDRS fuel model Y indices from the FEMS public climatology services, pulled live each time the page loads; PSA boundaries from the NIFC Predictive Service Area service. Historical statistics: 4,950 North Ops large fire-days, 2012–2026 (VIIRS perimeters), with PSA SIG weather from FEMS. Thresholds are the median value on each PSA's large growth days. Concern levels describe how similar conditions are to past large growth days; they are not a forecast of fire behavior.
